Pricing FAQ

Common questions about our plans

Can I change plans later? +
Yes. You can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time. Upgrades take effect immediately and we'll prorate the difference; downgrades take effect at the start of your next billing cycle.
What happens if I exceed my monthly limit? +
We won't block your operation. Overages are billed at the end of the month at the published per-application rate for your tier. You'll receive a notification when you reach 80% of your limit.
Is there a free trial? +
Yes — the Starter plan includes a 14-day free trial with no credit card required. For Professional and Enterprise, contact our team to schedule a guided demo and trial setup.
How does annual billing work? +
Annual billing is charged once per year and saves 20% compared to monthly billing. You can switch between monthly and annual at any time.
Do you offer discounts for NGOs or startups? +
We offer discounted pricing for qualifying NGOs, SACCOs, and early-stage lending startups. Reach out to our sales team through the support page to discuss your situation.
What payment methods do you accept? +
We accept bank transfers, mobile money, and all major credit cards. Enterprise customers can request invoicing with net-30 terms.
